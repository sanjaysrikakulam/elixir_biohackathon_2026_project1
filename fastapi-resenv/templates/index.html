How to register your cluster in Galaxy

Use the details above to register your Slurm cluster as an external compute resource in Galaxy (bring your own compute) — no dedicated Galaxy cloud site required:

1
Copy the PULSAR credentials
Use the copy buttons to grab the PULSAR Username and PULSAR Password for your cluster.
2
Open your Galaxy instance
Log in to your Galaxy instance where you want to run jobs on this cluster.
3
Add the PULSAR endpoint
In Galaxy, configure the cluster as a remote / PULSAR execution target and paste the credentials into the respective fields.
4
Match your resource limits
Keep your job requests within the Allowed Max CPUs, RAM and GPUs shown above.
Tip: If Galaxy cannot reach your cluster, verify that your network allows traffic to the PULSAR service and that the credentials above are current.
